Hakuba Happo eat map · 01–29 from Happo BT

DESCRIPTION
Numbers match The Ultimate JAPOW Chaser's Guide — Hakuba Happo chapter.

Layers are how you leave Happo Bus Terminal — not cuisine, not a ranking.

01–10  Walk from Happo BT. 10 Ringoya is lunch only.
11–16  Echoland (Line VN). Ride Line VN (~10 min). Do not walk from Happo (dark, ice). Sleep stays at Happo.
17–29  Taxi — station road, Misorano, Goryu / Kamishiro, Omachi car day-trip. 22 Grindel is lunch only. 28 Yamashina (~30 min by car) is not this week's must. 29 Riki is Mizuho lunch soba. Not a steakhouse.

Do not mix these pins:
• 06 Koiya (eel, Happo walk) ≠ 15 KoiKoi (izakaya, Echoland)
• 06 Koiya address is Happo 4093-2 (北城八方4093-2) — not 4093-2 Hokujo alone
• 19 Miyama is station yakiniku — not the old Echoland jingisukan (moved to Matsumoto)
• 09 Zen Soba ≠ 10 Ringoya ≠ 29 Riki ≠ 28 Yamashina — four soba shops
• 16 koubo-nikki is 3020-142 — not TABITABI (27) at 3020-1406
• 02 HAKUBAR is 5078 — not Sushi Sakai / THE HAPPO at 5090-1
• 07 Yohei is Happo 5057 — not 5069 / Taigakukan 5094
