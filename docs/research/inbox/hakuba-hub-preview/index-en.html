Hakuba Valley is a major inbound name, so it draws a crowd. Powder mornings here track out faster than on a quiet local hill. What still outweighs that: several mountains without a car, a municipal soak after the lifts, a walkable night, and one bus knot that holds a car-free week together.

This chapter is for a week of skiing, a soak, and nights out. Sleep at Happo Bus Terminal. Stay inside about a 10-minute walk. Echoland is the louder night; Wadano is quieter. Treat either as an evening, not as the bed.

Then you move. If you have never stayed in Nozawa Onsen, go—even though it is a major name too. Happo-One is the home port. Nozawa is Act 2.

Happo Onsen — after the hill

Ski, then soak, then dinner. Official: hakuba-happo-onsen.jp/english · analysis. Alkaline simple hot spring (hypotonic). Official: pH 11.2 (11.6 at analysis), source 49.7°C, minerals 128.2 mg/kg. Serpentine rock + hydrothermal water. Slick in the tub; skin feels silky after. Official: Japan’s only natural hydrogen hot spring. That is why you go — tired legs, then dinner. One night: soak from about 16:00, then Echoland (Eat). FAQ: “People with tattoos are welcome.” Hotel in-house baths: ask desk. No swimsuit. No municipal sauna.

Four municipal baths, same source. Default this week: Happo-no-Yu — largest, open-air, Happo walk. Then dinner.

Once you are at Happo

Daytime shuttles to Happo-One / Iwatake / Tsugaike are mostly free. Cortina is the exception: Line V ¥800 unless you already have a Hakuba Valley lift pass. Night bus is with dinner.

Ski bags: TA-Q-BIN airport → hotel ~¥2,500 / bag. Keep boots on you. Fair weather is usually next day. Snow / storm days can take 2–3 days. At the airport counter, ask which arrival day they are quoting — do not assume tomorrow. Yamato does not let you pick a delivery date on ski bags to a hotel. Lift tickets: buy Hakuba Valley online the night before. Do not buy in the morning maze. How-to.

Optional — rental car

Built without a car. Rent only for Cortina on your clock, or four adults splitting a van after a dump. Drinking and driving in Japan is a serious crime—not “dangerous,” not a fine you shake off. If you go out, you do not drive.

If you do rent, book at the airport you land, then drive to Happo (allow ~5 hr in winter). That replaces the Nagano Snow Shuttle. It is not an extra on top of Plan A.

Must — hotel parking, both stays. Happo and Nozawa inn lots are small. Reserve a space with the hotel before you pick up the car — Happo and Nozawa. Street parking in the village is not the backup. If either inn says full, you do not have a car plan. This is hotel parking, not the Happo-One ski-area lot.

Must — studless tires (スタッドレス). 4WD is not enough. Confirm studless on the booking screen and at pickup: look at the tires before you leave the lot. Airport cars often come on summer or all-season rubber unless you tick winter (sometimes extra). Chains in the trunk are not a substitute.

Book: Discover Cars — Haneda (HND) · Discover Cars — Narita (NRT). Winter 4WD is not the $23 banner. [VERIFY 2026–27]

7 nights — Happo then Nozawa

Five nights in a Happo-walk bed. Then you move. Two nights in Nozawa. Fly out the next morning — 8 calendar days, 7 nights.

Tonight (every Happo night except the last): japowsearch.com → wind on the Happo ridge → weather table → first bus you can board. This table is for no car, Happo BT. If Cortina tomorrow, buy Line V tonight and confirm that morning’s bus is running. If tomorrow is Tsugaike trees, watch the TSUGA POW DBD lecture tonight — the gate will not wait while you watch it at the summit.

If tonight looks like… Go tomorrow
Deep new snow + high wind Tsugaike (free Line T). Cortina only if that morning’s shuttle is running. Buses dead → Nakiyama.
Bluebird after the dump Happo-One ridge — if those lifts open. First chairs.
Rain / thaw in the village Go high (Happo / Tsugaike / Goryu Alps Daira). Shade: 47. Not Toomi.
No new snow Goryu Waves only in season (late Jan–late Mar). Else Happo groomers.
Weekend + fresh snow Norikura or Tsugaike. Skip Happo and Cortina. Iwatake = afternoon leftover.

Days 3–5 are not a locked tour of Iwatake → Cortina → 47. The weather table wins. Quiet fallbacks below are for a midweek week with no storm. Evening jobs are scarce: do not stack Hie and Goryu night skiing. Do not night-ski the night you land or the night you pack.

Tsugaike DBD is not “show up and ski trees.” Groomers need nothing extra. Echoland — only if you go out

Happo already has dinner and bars on foot (the list above). Echoland is the louder pocket — worth one night. Ride Line VN (Hakuba Night Bus) from Happo BT; about 10 minutes. Sleep stays at Happo.

Skip the Goryu night skiing and you want a louder night out: Echoland. Ride Line VN from Happo BT — about 10 minutes. Do not walk there from Happo (dark, ice, ~25–30 min). Once you are off the bus, that pocket is on foot. Ski from the morning, soak from about 16:00 (Happo-no-Yu, tattoo OK; last in 20:30), change in the room, then the bus. Inn dinner included? Tell the inn the day before you do not need dinner tomorrow.

Change in the room first. Skis and boards stay there. This is a dinner bus, not a hill shuttle — no ski or snowboard on the bus (official: limited space).

Adult ¥500 / child ¥250 (2025–26), online or cash. Buy the ticket before dinner. Last bus around 22:00 last season (2024–25 PDF). 2026–27 timetable not posted yet [VERIFY 2026–27]. Act 2 — Nozawa two nights

Do not day-trip this village from Happo. Move once. Fly out from Nozawa Chuo Terminal. Going back to Happo for the airport wastes a day.

The two nights are so you ski, then soak. A day-trip from Happo gets you the hill and misses the village.

Hakuba was the valley: several mountains, the shuttle brings the next hill to you. Nozawa does not give you that circuit. You get one long hill and one walkable onsen town — soto-yu after the last run, dinner on foot. Sleep in the village core (Chuo BT / Ōyu). Not Nagasaka ski-in (uphill ice after dinner). Not the outskirts.

Book a village bed — official desks first

Soto-yu

13 free village baths, run by neighbourhood bath associations. Official: natural spring, untreated, no added water. Typical winter hours 06:00–23:00 (ask the desk — Shin-yu closes earlier; Taki-no-yu is village-guest only). Bring soap and a towel. Wash and rinse before you get in. Shared village rooms, not a spa.

Official pages (as of 15 Aug 2026): start in English at the ski-portal trail — en.nozawaski.com/the-village/activities/onsen-trail. The fuller page is Japanese: source temps, hours per bath, map, manners — nozawakanko.jp/hotspring. They are two official sites, not a language toggle.
